Guide · Tolerances

Specify tolerance by function,
not by habit.

A dimension without a reason behind it is a cost you agreed to without knowing it.

Start from the general tolerance block

ISO 2768 exists so you do not have to dimension every edge. Declare a class in the title block, then call out only the features that break out of it.

ISO 2768 classLinear 0.5–3 mm6–30 mm120–400 mmUse for
f (fine)±0.05±0.1±0.2Precision assemblies, mating parts
m (medium)±0.1±0.2±0.5General machined parts
c (coarse)±0.2±0.5±1.2Weldments, non-critical brackets

Values are the standard's tabulated linear tolerances for the size bands shown. Angular and geometric general tolerances follow the same class.

Where precision earns its price

Bearing seats and shafts

A 6205 bearing has a 25 mm bore. Press fit into a housing usually calls for an H7 bore; a rotating shaft for h6 or j6 depending on load. Move 0.02 mm off those and you get either a bearing that spins in its seat or one you cannot install without heat.

Sealing grooves

Groove depth controls compression; side walls control extrusion. Depth is the one that leaks when it is wrong — a 0.05 mm deep groove reduces compression by roughly 1.5 percentage points on a 3.5 mm section.

Datum-referenced hole patterns

Position tolerance of ⌀0.1 at MMC buys you a functional gauge and interchangeable parts. Applying it to a cover plate nobody measures is money spent on paperwork.

When GD&T pays for itself

Geometric tolerancing is not decoration. It pays when a part is:

  • located by two or more features at once (position, profile)
  • rotating (coaxiality, runout)
  • sealing against a flat face (flatness, perpendicularity)

It does not pay on a single-sided bracket held by two bolts. There, a ±0.2 general tolerance and a note to deburr is both cheaper and clearer.

Common specification error: stacking a tight linear tolerance on a feature that also carries a geometric control. You pay twice for the same requirement. Pick the one that describes the function.

Surface finish, translated

CalloutWhat the shop doesAdds cost?
Ra 6.3Roughing pass onlyNo
Ra 3.2Standard finishing passNo
Ra 1.6Light finishing, slower feedSlightly
Ra 0.8Fine finishing or grindingYes
Ra 0.4Grinding / lappingSignificantly

Rule of thumb: sealing and sliding faces need Ra 1.6 or better. Static locating faces at Ra 3.2 are fine. Cosmetic outer faces at Ra 3.2 plus a bead blast look better than Ra 0.8 and cost less.
